Meetings and agendas

The quorum court meets the second Thursday of each month at 6:00 p.m. in the Quorum Court Room, 2nd floor of the courthouse. Every meeting is open to the public.

Coming up

Arkansas law (Act 505 of 2025) has the county post each meeting's time, place, date and current agenda online at least 3 days before a regular meeting, and at least 2 hours before a special or emergency meeting. Items can be added after posting; the stamp shows when.
