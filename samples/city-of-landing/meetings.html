Meetings and agendas

City council, planning commission and parks board meetings are open to everyone. The City posts each agenda here at least 3 days before a regular meeting and at least 2 hours before a special meeting, as state law requires.

Clerks: see the posting tool (staff view, sample)

Regular meetings

City council
2nd Monday of each month, 6:00 p.m., Council Chambers, City Hall
Planning commission
1st Tuesday of each month, 5:30 p.m., Council Chambers, City Hall
Parks and recreation board
3rd Thursday of each month, 5:30 p.m., Depot Park Community Building, 400 Depot Street

Taking part

  • To speak during public comment, sign up with the City Clerk before the meeting starts. Each person has 3 minutes.
  • Every meeting is recorded with sound. State law requires the City to keep each recording at least 1 year. Recordings from the past year are below.
  • Need an interpreter or another accommodation? Call the City Clerk at (870) 555-0134 at least 2 business days before the meeting.
  • Minutes are posted after the board approves them at its next meeting. The signed originals stay with the City Clerk.
