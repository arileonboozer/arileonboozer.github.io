Services

What each service includes, about how long it takes and what it costs. The prices are the same as on the board in the shop.

Or walk in, up to 30 minutes before closing.

Haircuts

Haircut

$25

About 30 minutes

Clippers, scissors or both, the way you want it. We line up the edges and finish with a hot towel and a neck shave.

Haircut with a beard trim

$35

About 45 minutes

Your haircut, plus the beard shaped to match: trimmed, lined with a straight razor and finished with a hot towel.

Skin fade

$30

About 45 minutes

Faded down to the skin on the sides and back, and blended into the length on top. Wes does most of our fades.

Kids' haircut, 12 and under

$18

About 20 minutes

Any style. We don't use a razor on kids, so the neckline is finished with clippers. There's a booster seat for little ones, and a parent is welcome to stand close.

Haircut, 65 and up

$20

About 30 minutes

The same haircut, hot towel and neck shave, any day we're open. Just tell us; we don't ask for ID.

Beards and shaves

Hot-towel shave

Beard trim

$15

About 20 minutes

Line-up

About 15 minutes

A cleanup between haircuts: the hairline, around the ears and the back of the neck.

Good to know

Not sure what to ask for?

Bring a photo, or tell us what you have now and what you'd change. We'll say what we'd do, and what it costs, before we start.

What we don't do

No color, perms or chemical straightening. Ask us, and we'll tell you who in town does them.
