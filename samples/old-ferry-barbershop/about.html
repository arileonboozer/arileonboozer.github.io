About the shop

Old Ferry Barbershop sits in the old ferry ticket office at the foot of Front Street. The chairs face the window, so you can watch the river while you wait.

How we ended up in the ticket office

For a long time the ferry was the only way across the river at Landing. When the bridge opened upriver, the ferry stopped running, and its ticket office at the foot of Front Street sat empty.

Wes rented it, patched the roof and put 2 chairs where the ticket counter used to be. They face the big window, so you can watch the river while you wait.

The ferry's rate board still hangs by the door. It used to list the fare for a car, a wagon and a person on foot. Now it lists ours.

Marisol took the second chair, and the shop has spoken 2 languages ever since.

Spanish spoken

Ask for Marisol when you book or walk in. She can go over every service and price in Spanish, and this website has a Spanish version too.

How a visit goes

  1. Walk in and put your name on the clipboard by the door, or book a time ahead.
  2. Tell us what you want, or show us a photo.
  3. We cut, line up the edges and finish with a hot towel and a neck shave. Most haircuts take about 30 minutes.
  4. Pay at the chair, cash or card.
