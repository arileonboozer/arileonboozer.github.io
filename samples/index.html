Slack Water Catfish

A riverside restaurant with a scroll moment and online ordering.

  • Scroll slowly: the sun sets over the dock, then the recipe appears line by line. The hours and the call button stay put.
  • Order for pickup: menu prices, quantities and a running total, pickup times only when the restaurant is open, and pay at pickup or by card on Stripe's own page.

Custom Build for a city or town · from $2,999.99

City of Landing

A small city's website, with tools for city hall staff.

  • Meetings: each agenda as a web page with the date and time it was posted, plus minutes and recordings.
  • A posting tool for the clerk that builds the agenda item by item, works out the deadline (3 days before a regular meeting, 2 hours before a special one, under Act 505 of 2025), warns when it's late and stamps it “Posted”.
  • Unpublishing never deletes anything; every change shows in a history.
  • Report a problem with a request number to check later, and a trash pickup day lookup.
  • Alerts on every page, the council by ward, departments, ordinances, public notices and records requests.

For a city, town or county there's no deposit: 1 invoice on delivery, due within 30 days. Arkansas cities and towns must use a .gov address for their website (Act 929 of 2025); those under 10,000 people by January 1, 2027.

Custom Build for a county · from $2,999.99

Sweetgum County

A rural county's website, built around the courthouse offices and the quorum court.

  • A property tax estimate that shows each step: assessed value, millage and the homestead credit.
  • Road closures and flooded low-water crossings, posted by the road department.
  • Quorum court agendas, minutes and recordings you can search, each with the time it was posted, and the county budget online, as Arkansas law asks (Act 564 of 2019).
  • A burn ban notice on every page, the elected offices, elections and records requests.

For a city, town or county there's no deposit: 1 invoice on delivery, due within 30 days.
