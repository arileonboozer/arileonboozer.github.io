Privacy

This is a sample site, so this page stands in for the real one.

A Custom Build that takes orders keeps customers' details, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses and orders, so it gets its own privacy page, written for the business. It says what is stored, who can see it, how long it is kept, which outside services handle it (such as Stripe for card payments) and how to ask for it to be deleted.

This sample stores nothing. Its order form runs only in your browser, sends nothing, charges nothing, sets no cookies and loads nothing from other websites.
