What we do

  • Shingle roofs

    Tear-off and a new shingle roof, usually in 1 to 3 days.

  • Metal roofs

    Standing-seam and screw-down metal for houses, shops and barns.

  • Repairs and leaks

    Leaks, missing shingles and flashing. We tarp first, then fix.

  • Storm damage

    Hail and wind damage. We tarp, photograph it and meet your adjuster.

  • Roof inspections

    A written report with photos, for a check-up, a sale or after a storm.

How a job goes

  1. 01

    We look

    We walk the roof and the attic and take photos. Free, usually within the week.

  2. 02

    Written quote

    You get a written quote within 2 business days, with the materials named.

  3. 03

    We set a date

    We order materials and pick a dry stretch of days together.

  4. 04

    Tear-off and install

    Old roof off, decking checked, new roof on. Most houses take 1 to 3 days.

  5. 05

    Cleanup and walk-through

    We run a magnet over the yard for nails and walk the job with you.
