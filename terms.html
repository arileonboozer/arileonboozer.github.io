7. Care Plan

The Care Plan is $29.99 per month, billed monthly in advance starting on the launch date, and runs month to month. You can cancel any time. It is for a site I built for you, unless we agree otherwise in writing. It covers looking after your hosting account and settings, edits within 3 business days of your request, backups, uptime checks and security checkups. Edits are changes to your existing pages, such as text, photos and hours. A new page or a redesign is quoted separately. Hosting for a site I build is on a free plan, so it has no fee. If your site ever uses a paid plan, you pay that fee directly.

Backups are copies of your site's files that I keep and update each time I change your site. If a problem breaks your site, I put back my most recent copy as soon as I can. Putting a copy back does not find or fix what caused the problem, and it does not include anything held by other companies, such as the messages in your email.

Security checkups are 3 jobs. First, I keep automatic alerts on your site's HTTPS certificate and your domain's renewal date, and I tell you when either needs attention. You own the domain and pay its renewal. Second, if your contact form sends messages through your website (rather than opening the visitor's own email), I keep its spam filter turned on. No filter catches everything, and it can block a real message. Your email provider can also send form messages to spam, so check the spam folder of the email address that receives your form messages. Third, about every 6 months I go through the logins tied to your site with you: your hosting account, your domain, any email addresses I set up on your domain and any Google Business Profile. I check whether 2-step sign-in is on and help you turn it on where it is not (it needs your phone), and I remove a user only when you tell me to. If you do not answer my request, I skip that round and ask again at the next one. You sign in yourself, or we look together on a shared screen, so I do not need your passwords or your 2-step codes for the review. You stay the owner of every account and can remove me whenever you like. If you cancel the plan, I remove my own access.

Checkups are routine upkeep, not a guarantee. They do not promise that a site or account will never be broken into, taken over or misused. Not part of the plan: a security audit, finding out how a break-in happened, cleaning up a hacked site, looking after a site I did not build, and the security of your phones, computers and networks.
