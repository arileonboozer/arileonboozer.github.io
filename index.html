You can pay monthly ($29.99), every 3 months ($89.97) or yearly ($359.88). The price is the same each way, with no discount for paying ahead. When you cancel, the plan runs to the end of the month already underway, and if you paid for a quarter or a year, I refund every full month after that. For a Custom Build, the plan covers the website as for a Full Site; changes or upkeep for the system I built (its data, sign-ins and scheduled emails) are quoted in writing. Security checkups are routine upkeep, not a guarantee: they do not promise that a site or account will never be broken into, taken over or misused. If a problem breaks your site, I put back my most recent saved copy of its files as soon as I can; that does not find or fix the cause. A security audit, finding out how a break-in happened, cleaning up a hacked site, looking after a site I did not build, and the security of your phones, computers and networks are not part of the plan.

Common questions

Do you offer services to nonprofits?

Yes. Nonprofits and other tax-exempt organizations get 20% off everything, including the Care Plan and hourly edits. If you pay in full up front, the $50, $100 or $150 comes off after the 20%. Costs I pass along at cost, like a domain, aren't discounted. I confirm your status with the IRS using your EIN. Government bodies are not included; they get the pay-at-delivery terms instead.

Do you offer services to governments?

Yes. I build websites for cities, towns, counties and other public bodies. No deposit is needed: work starts when I receive the signed agreement or your purchase order. I send 1 invoice for the full price on delivery, due within 30 days. If your office uses its own contract or purchase order form, I can sign that instead.

What is the AI Consultant Report?

A short written report on how AI could help your business. You answer 8 short written questions and pay $99.99, and within 3 business days of your payment I send the 2 or 3 tasks to fix first, worked out from your numbers, the tools to use (starting with what you already pay for), costs and risks, what not to buy yet, and a do-it-yourself plan. It comes by email, with the report also attached as a Word document and as PowerPoint slides at no extra cost, in case you want to present it. I write it myself, using AI-assisted tools, and check it before it goes to you. Before any AI-assisted tool sees your answers, I take out customer names, phone numbers, addresses, card numbers and health details. It is written for a small business, such as a trade, auto shop, restaurant, salon, shop, office, farm, church or nonprofit. A health office gets a narrower report, and a business with several locations is not a fit. Questions about your report are answered in writing for 14 days after I send it. It is advice only, and it is not legal, tax or compliance advice.

What if I already have a Facebook page?

Keep it. I will build links to your social media pages into your website. A website can show up in Google searches, and it works for people who are not on Facebook. Maps shows a business's Google Business Profile, which is a separate listing from its website.

How is it this fast?

I build with AI-assisted tools, and I review and test everything myself before it ships. The scope is small and the price is fixed. That is why the turnaround is fast.

What do you need from me?

Written answers to a short questionnaire, which I send by email or text after your first payment (about 15 minutes, plus finding photos). Your photos and logo if you have them. An email address you check, which becomes your login for the website accounts I set up in your name. If you already own a domain, a way for me to change a setting where it is registered: you add me as a user there, or I send you short written steps. Please don't send me that password. If you don't have a domain, I can register it for you, and for that I also need your name, a mailing address and a mobile number. Either way, a few minutes to click the confirmation emails the account companies send you. A Google account for your business. If you don't have one, I create one in your name: Google texts a code to your mobile phone, and you send that code to me right away. When I deliver, you set your own password and turn on 2-step sign-in. Your Google Business Profile and, with a Full Site, your Google Search Console and Bing accounts are set up in your name. If you already have a profile and I am doing the makeover, you invite me to it as a manager. For an AI Consultant Report, I only need your answers to the questionnaire, which you send before you pay.

Do I own the site?

Yes. The finished site becomes yours on full payment. I set up your hosting account, and your domain if you need one, in your name, and you take control of them when I deliver. When the balance is paid, I also send you a copy of your site's files. Your content stays yours.

What does it cost after launch?

A domain is typically about $15 a year. If I register it for you, I add the first year to your invoice at cost, and after that you pay the registrar directly. Your hosting, your contact form and, with a Full Site, your visitor analytics are set up on free plans, so there is no other monthly fee. If you add a mobile app, Apple charges a developer fee to keep the iPhone version on the App Store, currently $99 a year. I cover the first year, and after that you pay Apple directly. If a Custom Build takes card payments, the card company keeps a small fee from each sale. The Care Plan is optional.

What if my web address already has email?

Your email keeps running where it is. When your web address (domain) moves to your new hosting, I carry its email settings over and check that your mail still arrives. Your contact form then opens a filled-in email in the visitor's own email app for them to send. If you want a form that sends straight from your site, with a spam check, I set it up on a second web address that only the form uses. That address has its own yearly domain fee, typically about $15.

Why a map link and not a map on the page?

Your pages load no maps, fonts, videos or ads from other companies. The link opens Google Maps when someone taps it.

Do you build for accessibility?

Every site I build gets accessibility basics: readable text, strong color contrast, image descriptions and headings in order. It is not a full review against a standard such as WCAG 2.1 AA.

Can reminders go by text?

My builds send reminders by email, which has no fee. Business texting needs a paid texting service and registration with the phone carriers, which come with monthly fees.

What if I need changes later?

Edits are included in the Care Plan, up to 30 minutes of work a month, made within 3 business days of your request. More time, or edits without the plan, are billed at a flat $30 an hour.
