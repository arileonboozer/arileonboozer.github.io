How it works

Three steps, start to finish.

  1. 1

    We trade a few messages

    I send you a short list of questions by email or text: what you do, your hours and where to find you. You reply in writing and send me your photos, and your logo if you have one. I write the words from your answers.

  2. 2

    I build it

    A custom design for your business, not a template. I build with AI-assisted tools, then review and test everything myself before it ships. A one-page site is ready to review within two business days of receiving your content.

  3. 3

    You review it and it goes live

    You look it over and tell me what to change. One round of revisions is included with a Launch Page, two with a Full Site. As soon as you approve it and the balance is paid, I connect the site to your domain and it goes live.

Questions

Common questions

How is it this fast?

I build with AI-assisted tools, and I review and test everything myself before it ships. The scope is small and the price is fixed. That is why the turnaround is fast.

What is the AI Review?

A short written report on how AI could help your business. You answer a written questionnaire, and within three business days of getting your answers I send three specific tasks AI could help with, the tools I would use, rough costs and risks, and a next step. I write it myself, using AI-assisted tools, and check it before it goes to you. It is advice only, and it is not legal, tax or compliance advice.

What do you need from me?

Short written answers to a few questions, by email or text. Your photos and logo if you have them. Access to your domain if you own one. If you don't have one, I can register it for you.

Do I own the site?

Yes. The finished site becomes yours on full payment. The domain and hosting are in your name, and your content stays yours.

What does it cost after launch?

A domain is typically about $15 a year, paid to the registrar. Hosting for a simple site is usually free or a few dollars a month. I can register the domain for you if you ask. The Care Plan is optional.

What if I already have a Facebook page?

Keep it. A website is what shows up in Google and Maps searches, and it works for people who are not on Facebook.

What if I need changes later?

Edits are included in the Care Plan, made within two business days of your request. Without the plan, small edits are billed at a flat $60 an hour.
