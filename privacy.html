Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information I collect, how I use it, who I share it with, and how I keep it safe. It covers this website (arileonboozer.com) and the web and software work I do for clients. I am Ari Leon Boozer, a sole proprietor based in Newport, Arkansas. In this policy, “I” means me.

What I collect

  • Messages you send me. When you text or email me, or use the contact form, I receive your name, your contact details and whatever you write. The form on this site does not send anything to a server. It opens a Gmail window in your browser with the message filled in, or your own email app if you choose that link, and nothing is sent until you press send there.
  • Project information. If you hire me, I receive the content, photos, logins and business details you give me so that I can do the work.
  • Payment information. Card payments are made on a Stripe invoice page. Your card number is entered on Stripe's page and never reaches me. I can see the invoice amount, your name and email, and whether it was paid. If you pay by Zelle, Cash App, check or cash, I see only what your bank or app shows me, such as your name and the amount.
  • Basic site data. This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it loads no fonts or scripts from other sites. It saves one setting, your light or dark theme choice, on your own device. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit, under its own privacy statement.

How I use it

  • To answer your messages and prepare quotes.
  • To build, deliver and support the work you hire me for.
  • To send invoices, take payment and keep the records that tax and business law require.

I do not sell your information, and I do not use it for advertising.

Who I share it with

I share information only when it is needed for the work, and only through the providers' own accounts and secure connections. Those are:

  • Stripe, to send invoices and process card payments.
  • The banks and apps you choose to pay with, such as Zelle or Cash App.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • A domain registrar, hosting provider or email provider, if you ask me to set up a domain, hosting or email addresses for you. They receive the contact details needed to register those accounts in your name.
  • My accountant and tax authorities, where tax rules require it.
  • Anyone the law requires me to tell, such as in response to a legal order.

I do not share your information with anyone else.

How I keep it safe

  • This site is served over HTTPS.
  • Only I have access to the information clients give me.
  • Card data stays with Stripe and never passes through me.
  • I use strong, unique passwords and two-step login on the accounts that hold your information, where the provider offers it.
  • Logins you give me for a project are used only for that project.

No method of storing or sending information is perfectly secure, so I cannot promise absolute security. If I learn that your information has been exposed, I will tell you.

How long I keep it

I keep project and invoice records for as long as I need them to support your site and to meet tax and legal record-keeping rules. Messages from people who never became clients can be deleted when you ask.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email me at boozer.ira@gmail.com. I may need to keep some records, such as invoices, to follow tax law.

Children

My services are for businesses and adults. I do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If I change this policy, I will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
