Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information I collect, how I use it, who else handles it, and what you can ask me to do. It covers this website (arileonboozer.com), the emails, texts and messages I send and receive for my business, and the work I do for clients, including websites, software and AI Consultant Reports. I am Ari Leon Boozer, a sole proprietor based in Newport, Arkansas. In this policy, “I” means me.

What I collect

  • Messages you send me. When you email me, text me, message me on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or X, or use the contact form, I receive your name, your contact details and whatever you write. Nothing reaches me until you press send.
  • The contact form. The contact form on this site does not send anything to my website or to any server I run. When you press the form’s button, it opens Gmail in a new tab with your message filled in. Everything you typed in the form (your name, business, phone number and message) goes to Google as part of the web link that opens Gmail, and Gmail may save it as a draft, even if you never press send. The page then also shows a link to open the same message in your own email app, but by then Gmail has already opened with your text, unless your browser blocked the new tab. If you use that link, your device opens your email program, or the webmail service your browser is set to use, with your message filled in. If it opens a webmail service, that service receives your text and may save it as a draft. If JavaScript is turned off in your browser, the form’s button skips Gmail and opens your email program, or the webmail service your browser is set to use, the same way as that link.
  • Email links. The Email buttons and the email address link in the page footer open your own email app, or the webmail service your browser is set to use, with only my email address and a subject line filled in. If you would rather use Gmail, the link next to “Prefer Gmail?” in the Contact section of the main page opens Gmail in a new tab, with only my email address and a subject line filled in. The plain email addresses on this page and on the Terms of Service page also open your own email app, or the webmail service your browser is set to use, but with only my email address filled in.
  • Text messages. If you tap a text button, your phone’s messaging app opens with a new message to (870) 201-0441 ready to write. Nothing is sent until you press send there. The message then travels through your phone’s own messaging service (your phone company, or an app such as iMessage) under that service’s rules, not through this website. I use your phone number and what you write to reply to you and to handle what you asked about. If you ask me to stop texting you, I will stop. You can ask me to delete our text conversation from my phone. I cannot delete copies that are on your phone or with your phone company. Sometimes I text or message someone first, such as a person I know or someone who asked me to follow up. I write each of those messages myself, individually, and if you ask me to stop, I will stop.
  • Project information. If you hire me or order an AI Consultant Report, I receive the content, photos, logins, questionnaire answers and business details you give me so that I can do the work. If I set up accounts for you, such as your domain, I also receive the name, mailing address, phone number and email address those accounts need. For some projects, such as online booking or ordering, a customer portal, or a data cleanup or migration, I also receive information about your customers.
  • Payment information. Card payments are made on a Stripe invoice page. Your full card number is entered on Stripe’s page and never reaches me. I can see details such as the invoice amount, your name and email, the card’s brand and last 4 digits, and whether it was paid. If you pay by Zelle or Cash App, I see what the app or my bank shows me, such as your name and the amount. If you pay by check, I see what is printed on it, which can include your name, address, bank, and routing and account numbers. If you pay in cash, I see no bank or app details.
  • Basic site data. This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising tools or social media plugins, and it loads no code, fonts or images from other companies’ servers. If you use the light or dark theme switch, the site saves your choice on your own device, in your browser’s local storage. GitHub hosts this site. Like any web host, it records technical details such as your IP address when you visit, under its own privacy policy.
  • Links to other sites. This site links to my pages on Facebook, LinkedIn, X and Instagram, and to NeoLibrary (neolibrary.net), an app I built, and to a Google help page about working with a third party. These are ordinary links. Nothing is shared with those companies unless you tap one. When you do, you leave this site and that company’s own privacy policy applies.

Businesses I contact by email

I sometimes email local businesses to introduce my services. For that I use business contact details that are publicly posted, such as the email address on the business’s own website, on its Facebook page or in a public listing, along with the business name, the owner’s name if the business or a public listing shows it, and what I noticed about its website. I keep a list of who I have emailed and when, and notes on other local businesses I may write to. These emails say they are ads and show my mailing address. If you reply “stop,” or email or text me to stop, I will not email you again, and I keep a record of your request so that I do not email you by mistake.

How I use it

  • To answer your messages and prepare quotes.
  • To build, deliver and support the work you hire me for.
  • To show the finished website or app in my portfolio, unless you opt out in the project agreement.
  • To send invoices, take payment and keep the records that tax and business law require.
  • To introduce my services by email to local businesses, as described above.
  • To follow up by text or message with people I know or who asked me to.
  • To help me write and check code, web pages and reports, and to keep my records, including with AI tools.

I do not sell your information.

Who else handles it

These companies and people handle information as part of running this site and my business:

  • Google (Gmail), which runs my email account and so handles every email I send or receive. It also receives what you typed in the form when the contact form opens a Gmail window, even if you do not press send.
  • Your phone company or messaging app, if you send me a text.
  • Your email provider or webmail service, if you send me an email or use an Email button or email link.
  • Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or X, if you send me a message there. Each handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Stripe, to send invoices and process card payments.
  • The banks and apps used for your payment, such as Zelle or Cash App, including my own bank, which receives the payment.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • AI tools, such as Claude (from Anthropic) and Codex (from OpenAI), which I use to help write and check code, web pages and reports, and to help keep my invoices and records. They receive whatever I put into them or let them read, which can include parts of what you send me or give me for a project, your invoice and payment details, and the business details I use for my emails. Each handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the websites I build for clients, runs their contact forms and, for a Full Site, counts visits. If you hire me for a website, I create a Cloudflare account in your name with your email address, and Cloudflare receives that address and what I put into the account for your site, such as your business details, photos and the email address your form messages go to.
  • HetrixTools, which checks that the websites I build are up and warns before their HTTPS certificate or domain registration runs out. If your site is on my Care Plan, HetrixTools receives your website's address and sends its warnings to me. If it is not, I create a free HetrixTools account in your name with your email address, and HetrixTools receives that address and your website's address and sends its warnings to you.
  • A domain registrar, hosting provider or email provider, if you ask me to set up a domain, hosting or email addresses for you. They receive the contact details needed to register those accounts in your name.
  • Google and Microsoft (Bing), if I create a Google account for your business or set up your Google Business Profile, Google Search Console or Bing listings. They receive the details needed to set up those accounts in your name, such as your business details, the mobile number that receives Google's codes and the email address used to recover the account.
  • Apple and Google, if you hire me to build a mobile app and I set up the developer accounts for you. They receive the details needed to register those accounts in your name.
  • An accountant or tax preparer, if I use one, and tax authorities, where tax rules require it.
  • Anyone the law requires me to tell, such as in response to a legal order.

I share your information only with the companies and people listed here, and only as needed to run this site and my business.

Tracking and Do Not Track

This site has no advertising or analytics tools, and nothing on it is set up to track your activity across websites. Some web browsers can send a “Do Not Track” signal. Because this site does not track your activity on other websites, there is nothing for that setting to switch off, and the site does not change what it does when your browser sends one.

How I keep it safe

  • This site is served over HTTPS.
  • Apart from the companies and people listed above, I do not give anyone access to the information clients give me.
  • Your full card number stays with Stripe and never passes through me.
  • Logins you give me for a project, or for your Care Plan, are used only for that work.

Messages you send by email or text travel through your email provider or phone company, and are protected only as well as they protect them. No method of storing or sending information is perfectly secure, so I cannot promise absolute security.

How long I keep it

I keep emails and texts for as long as I need them to help you and to run my business. I keep project and invoice records for as long as I need them to support your site and to meet tax and legal record-keeping rules. If you ask me to stop contacting you, I keep your name and email address or phone number on a do-not-contact list so that I honor your request, even if you also ask me to delete everything else. Messages from people who never became clients can be deleted when you ask.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, ask me to delete it, or ask me to stop emailing or texting you. Email me at ari@arileonboozer.com or text me at (870) 201-0441. I may ask you to confirm it is you. I can change only what I hold: I cannot change copies held by your email provider, your phone company or other companies. I may need to keep some records, such as invoices, to follow tax law. If you have asked me to stop contacting you, I also keep your name and email address or phone number on a do-not-contact list so that I honor your request.

Children

My services are for businesses and adults. This site is for a general audience, not for children. If you are a parent and think your child sent me information through this site, tell me and I will delete what I hold. I cannot delete copies held by email providers, phone companies or other companies.

Changes

If I change this policy in a way that matters, I will post the new version here and change the date at the top.
